# Futurist Moral Re-Framing Thought Experiment
I think the modern left is obsessed with "Being on the right side of history" it stems from an insecurity of being able to do the right thing and trying to find the best moral high horse. I think it's fair and not just us on that side on the compass, but the way it's gone about is wrong.

I think, we're in a rush to create this moral utopia. We want to be the ones to solve it and we hate the idea of being wrong in the eyes of history.

Yet the rush to have all the answers and not try to find how we get there is making it worse.

Let's live, do our best, because I think a truly compassionate human will also be able to see and forgive the past, especially with where we are now at least trying.
