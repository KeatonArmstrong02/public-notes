# Understanding mediums is necessary for mastery

Using [[Bloom's Taxonomy]] [[Levels of thinking]] as a frame, we understand that evaluation is the highest level of thinking that allows for integration. Lower levels is knowledge and comprehension

^Come back to later after we've seen it ourselves,

----
From what we saw in [[Personal development is stuck at the tactical level within the greater medium]] it reminded me of Arthur Brown, that kid could understand knowledge but I don't think he had access to higher levels of thinking. He could never analyze or evaluate on his own the point of any of the personal development ideas he was reading at the time.

Personal development is like collecting tools but without understanding what the toolbox is, a medium, you're not able to express through it.


"Adobe _Illustrator_ is essentially different from any of the individual tools _Illustrator_ contains. Such a medium creates a powerful immersive context, a context in which the user can have new kinds of thought, thoughts that were formerly impossible for them."
