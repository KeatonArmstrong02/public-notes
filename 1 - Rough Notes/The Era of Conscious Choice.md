# The Era of Conscious Choice
The challenge required of man in the modern age is the development of his autonomy and centeredness within himself to manage the sea of seemingly infinite choices.

We have access to see and "know" about a larger range of values and beliefs about the world than ever before, and all of it demands that we make a choice. You are forced to [[Engineering Beliefs]]. If we do not know intimately what matters to us we can easily be swept up by alien values. 

This level of freedom is highly life-affirming, allowing a cultivated individual to express themselves to a fuller degree within their life. (See [[Existentialism]] and the Ubermencsh) 

However, "Without adequate self-esteem, the amount of choice offered to us today can be frightening." With all of the weight to make decisions, without self-efficacy and the ability to trust one's own mind, we are not open to many of the opportunities which will end up scaring us.

It is within low-esteem that we seek the safety of not having to think. One prefers having someone else decide for them because we do not want to be burdened with making the decision of what is right and what is wrong. We shrink away from responsibility out of a feeling of inadequacy.

T. George Harris
