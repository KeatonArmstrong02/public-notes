# I'm a slow thinker
I got my ass kicked in math bowls in the 3rd grade. Though, I had decent grades I wasn't able to calculate at the raw speed of many other kids.

In high school, I learned that I read pretty slow too, as me and some friends had the same assignment, I got nervous seeing how they'd turn the page much faster than I would.

As an adult, if you debate me, I'm likely to be swayed by what you say. (particularly if I don't have much of an opinion already) I'll finally have a reply a few days later after turning it over in my head more.

When I read, I have to consciously slow down often because I'll finally make sense of the last idea when I'm halfway through the next.
