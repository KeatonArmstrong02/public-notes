# Tightening grip on the jacket

There's a taoist story about the wind and sun's wager to get a man to remove his coat.

The wind is boastful and believes in his strength, so he blows and blows but the man hunkers down and holds on to his jacket ever tighter because of the wind.

The sun, on his turn, simply shined and allowed for the man to remove his coat in the heat.

I think there's a mix of two concepts in dealing with people here.
1. You have to create a genuine want in the other person to influence them.
2. Attacking with more force only shuts away who we're talking with, you must break down the walls before you can get anything to them. See [[Communication with guards up is pointless]]
