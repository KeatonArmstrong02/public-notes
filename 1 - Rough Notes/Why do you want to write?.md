Following https://www.henrikkarlsson.xyz/p/writing-to-think
and https://www.cold-takes.com/learning-by-writing/

I want to write because I think it can help people break out of the lies we're told about how to live

- What Lies?

- school is how you learn things
- The things we need to be happy and culture's push for them (jobs, degrees, materials, dreams, goals)
- The lie we tell ourselves that we know how to think, I want to expose how we can truly think better for intellectual, spiritual, and relational reasons.
- The lie that we're stuck with this view culture gives us
- We all live in the exact same world, how does it end up SO different for all of us? How can we come to eunderstand that?

Okay so obviously quite broad, can we enhance the stance on any of these?



Better ideas have drastically improved my life. Finding better ways of thinking about things helped me model better thinking. Only through doing this a lot, do you start to develop the meta-skill of better thinking in general: What is it? Learning how to navigate information and eventually the goal is to be able to think for yourself. 

This improves your relationships, career, creative pursuits, spirituality, self-esteem, there is absolutely no domain in your life that learning to think better will not improve.

So perhaps a better way to write, instead of being stuck on the meta of writing/thinking better. I go back to writing on the practical. On specific ideas. They can be anecdotes to how my political views have changed, my relationship with Kendall and what models we use. My own research to improve things (Social + Self-esteem). The benefits of how it's helped me develop philosophy and understand myself better. (Breaking bad reasons anecdote).
