# The day our bubbles popped

Tangent from [[The fisher's aquarium]] which I took out as it disrupted my original idea, but spawned this idea for a short story. The following is that tangent

---
Just as the platforms have algortihmatized, the marketers had to figure out theirs as well. This is capture, this is hooks, this is "getting people to care" getting them to read, getting them to do anything. It's making a splash, it's putting yourself out there in a form which is recognizable to fight for attention.

Not everyone is kind with these tactics.
Users feel burned when they go down a journey, just to realize it was another funnel.
To realize that at the end of it was just another avenue into being told to buy something. 
Ah.. economics, our beautiful incentive for humanity.
The force that churns us into dollar signs, customers, clients, and prospects.

Because we've come to value the thousands of shallow relationships which earn us money far more than the few deep ones which take up so much of our time and offer us nothing. How could we possibly stand such *unprofitable* relationships of joy and emotion. They won't get you your dreams of vacation, they won't get you the safe neighborhood to raise your kids, they won't help you prove to your father that you are enough, they won't fulfill your fantasies. They are reality. Reality is boring. Leave me to my fantasies of having parasocial patrons instead of costly friends. 

I want a *community* I want an *audience* not the singular ol' Tony and Susie.
I believe in psychology, I'm a believer in the mass blankets which have simplified (finally) humanity into compressible packets of data for my programs to run on. Why would I be interested in wasting my time getting to know someone when I can write about their problems online and get them to pay me. We're all the same, my time is far more *leveraged* if I use the internet to *connect* with my thousands of users.%%Tweet of an influencer who talks about connection%%

Connection isn't a server. Connection is mother. Connection is choking each other out in an octagon. Connection is sipping coffee while you don't say a word. Connection is sex. Connection is meeting each others eyes. Connection is taking out your airpods. Connection is not looking down as you walk past. Connection is not rolling your window up at the beggars.

---

Fury. Madness. Dante's inferno. I'm watching the hell I created unfold. It was supposed to be heaven.

No one understands the world like I do. While the other shitters in my generation were playing on their hamster wheels of porn, memes, and ragebait, spamming their brains with flashes of emotion like zombies, thinking that it made them more human. I was building utopia.

It had always been alive in my head, seeing as clearly as I did you could tell I was one of the last humans alive while everyone else turned into an algorithm. They take their little inputs and react in a handful of ways. They don't see the prewiring of their personalities and how everything they think is original or "them" is just variation of a few slider scales on their traits.

I don't think I'm better than anyone, I'm not a narcissist. I was just born with the right mix of genes at the right time to become one of those few humans who had the chance to become god.
