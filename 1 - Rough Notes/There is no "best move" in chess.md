# There is no "best move" in chess

Frankl mentions in [[Man's Search for Meaning by Viktor Frankl]] clients would ask him, "What is the meaning I should aspire to?" It reminded him of asking what is the best move in chess.

A question which cannot be answered because there is a world of context needed. Even if we could come up with a single answer, we must be careful with [[Everything looks like a nail to one with a hammer]].

Learners will frequently ask this, "what is the best way to learn" in my experience for languages as well. (Perhaps a hack is starting with [[The one thing]])
