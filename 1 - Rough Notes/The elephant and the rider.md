# The elephant and the rider
The elephant and the rider is a metaphor that [[Johnathan Haidt]] uses in a few of his books, my exposure was within [[The Righteous Mind by Johnathan Haidt]].

The rider is our conscious, rational mind, he tries to steer the elephant. Closely related to [[System 2]]

The elephant is our unconscious mind, it makes up the large body of knowledge which affects our actions without us having to think about it, closely related with [[System 1]]. 

#### Reference
[[Johnathan Haidt]]
