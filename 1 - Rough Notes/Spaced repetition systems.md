# Spaced repetition systems

Spaced repetition is based on the forgetting curve for retrieval of information.

Apps like Anki and SuperMemo will give you flashcards and you can prompt back 4 degrees of ease. If you don't remember the card it will reset your timer--if you do the timer for that specific card will accumulate more and more distance in time before it shows you again.
