# You are the salesman of your esteem

Humans are wired so densely for sociability, we make snap judgments about people. These judgments, of course, won't have everything about you to go off of. Yet it is a mistake to believe there isn't already a plethora of information being provided. Regardless of interpretation, the facts of how you present yourself are real.

These interpretations have consequences, (Arbitrarily valued depending on the level) for your self-esteem.

When you see another human who does not value themselves, it is hard to incline yourself otherwise of their worth (they know themselves best, of course).

This can become a reinforcing cycle, particularly for those with low esteem, as they will take this signal from other's reactions to them as a reinforcement of their perceived low value.

For those of middling or higher esteem, they may take the positive reactions of others as reinforcement that they are deserving of such appraisal.
🚩Keep in mind this is particular for those with [[Pseudo-esteem]]🚩
