# Restart in the middle
You've learned the basics, you've learned from other people and now you're starting to connect things for yourself in your own mind.

This is when you need to restart.

Take everything you've captured and start fresh. Whatever you were using before to capture is probably messy and not so organized, you kept saving it for later.

This is how you get to the next level. You need a system.
