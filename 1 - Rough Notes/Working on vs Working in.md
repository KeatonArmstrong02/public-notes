# Working on vs Working in

There's a concept that's been helpful for small business owners that is the cost of most of them failing.

It is the energy requirements of working ON something vs working IN something.

If you're stuck working in your business you never have the capacity to work on your business, to improve your operating procedures and efficiency, to steer the thing where you want to take it.
