# Skepticism is the path to freedom

I wonder if there is a science to it? #question 

yet there does appear to be an art certainly. The only way to differentiate yourself in this world is skepticism. The absolute gigantism of prevailing ideas and beliefs in the world will suck you in by sheer gravity. You can move in their direction and agree with things, but only if you [[Do your own thinking]] are you able to disagree. (Understand when you're programmed to disagree as well. The opposing sides aren't opposites if you still dont think it through yourself)
Nuance is what saves you and opens up new pathways.

[[The conformity of the wannabes]]
You can believe that it is better not to be average, to be something different. It's easy to recognize that it's a failing system. Yet why do you believe it? Is it because someone you admire told you to? Getting the "right answer" in this case still doesn't make you any different, you're just following the slightly more right advice.

This is the process of [[Creating the misfit]] that [[James Dyson]] talked about. You're fighting your [[Social conditioning]] to become yourself again.
