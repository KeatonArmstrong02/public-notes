# Constraints are the fun part

I've heard many times in the conversation of "Which animal would you be for a day" The answer of a bird because of its "freedom of movement" how great it must feel to have the wind on you and to have full 3D movement ability. 

Don't fish have nearly the same level of freedom in their movement? They're confined to water you say. Okay, so perhaps its how birds can move in a way that we can't. Perhaps, but their legs are lame, they can't walk very fast and most of what they would want to do, flying is the better option.

The movement constraints we have as humans are the most fun things about us. Think about how few sports we would have as birds.


This might be the craziest thing I've ever written.
