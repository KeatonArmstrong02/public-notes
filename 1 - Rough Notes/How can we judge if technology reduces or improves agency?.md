We see the obvious effects we could have on our agency when it comes to outsourcing with AI. But the idea that they're more of an assisted handicap than helpful isn't true. In many cases it will improve our agency, just with a different result.

Contrast: Art for the sake of craft and art for the sake of existence

For example, I had a great idea for the sci-fi short story, I would have previously had to scrap, sell, or just poorly execute and never see this idea realized, to me, the fun was in the idea generation, not actually making it. My premise was the creativity, the writing of it is skill, that, I'll outsource to AI and I still get the final judgement, a final artist's filter on "Do I like it?"

In this case, I'm engaging in art for the sake of existence.
