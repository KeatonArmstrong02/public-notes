# Meta-learning kept me too pristine

Fear of doing something ineffectively must be balanced with a proper understanding of diminishing returns.

When you're obsessed with optimization, you start seeing that reality, not the reality of applying it. [[Venkatesh Rao]] says "When you have a hammer in your hand, everything looks like a nail. On the other hand, if you can’t see nails, all you see is opportunities to make better hammers."
