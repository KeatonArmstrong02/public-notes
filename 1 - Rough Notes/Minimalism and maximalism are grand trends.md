Minimalism is Hemmingway, it is Apple design, It is simple.
Minimalism is the assembly-line, it is cars after the 50's, it is replacable, smooth parts. It is ikea.

Maximalism is the Morrocan interior architecture. Maximalism is the hand-carved details all over antique furniture, it is the way Shakespeare writes, it is the Victorian era.

I wonder what I could possibly be writing, were I to be born in a maximalist era on the cusp of a culture shift into minimalism. However I feel the inverse. I'm surrounded by minimalism, I hate it AND I love it, it's ingrained in my taste. It makes me swoon and it makes me shudder.

It isn't necessarily the aesthetics which makes me shudder but the implications it has on consumers. See [[The impact of minimalism on modern audiences]]
