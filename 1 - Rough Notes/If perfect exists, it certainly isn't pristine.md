# If perfect exists, it certainly isn't pristine

Some of the ways we see "Perfect" involves a pristine mountain-top with serene nature surrounding it. As an athlete, someone who's body is in the "perfect" state isn't completely unhindered. It's only after the body has warmed up it's muscles and worked a bit that it's now in its best form.

Perfect isn't statuesque, it's when it is in complete kinetic completion of it's role.

A better word for how most use "perfect" is pristine. If i had to rework what genuine perfection is, I might just call it whole.
