[[Pareto's Principle]] is an incredible frame for aiming your productivity. If you are a marketer, you want to aim at the largest 80% of people you can while not having to produce as much.

However, I've become disgruntled with its application within art, entertainment, and marketing. By aiming at the larger audience, you don't aim at the 20%. This is fine, you can't cater to everyone, but most still try to cater to a larger audience than not.

By writing everything at the level of a 3rd grader, more people will understand.
But now no one has the opportunity to be challenged and increase their reading level.

Edutainment creators are constantly catering for beginner audiences, and assume they have a percentage funnel. but audiences aren't static, they're dynamic.

We see with [[Hormozi's case study]] how finding the 20% of his audience who account for 80% of his results is what he needs to aim at. Therefore, he'll double down on making content for a minority of his audience which brings in a majority of what he is looking for.

The best student isn't the one who needs to be catered to, the best student is the one who had drive anyways.
Then, if they have drive, why would they hire you?
