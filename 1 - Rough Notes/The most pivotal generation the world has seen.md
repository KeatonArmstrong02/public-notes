# The most pivotal generation the world has seen

Gen Z is in a position that humanity has never witnessed on a global scale. We are displaced, we are consumed by the world that was "created for us" we were told that we have it better than it's ever been had. We've held the world of information at our fingertips. 



There has never been a generation of people so suffocated by airlessness. And we don't even have the emotion to respond to it. We have a mass addiction to something none of the rest of us understand.
