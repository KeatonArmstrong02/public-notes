# You don't NEED consistency, it's just more efficient

If I told you to get 1 million views on a single video, I bet I could do it. I would do something absolutely ridiculous. Hitchhike from Alaska to Chile or Europe to China. I would build a Rube-goldberg machine.

but I don't *want* to do those things.

If you told me to get a million views quickly, it would change how I would do it than if you asked if I could get a million views a bunch of time, or even turn it into an infinite game like, *as many views as possible*.

Having skill is backup for performance. It's more solid. We fear putting a ton of effort into one project, when many times doing a bunch of projects is better. (See [[Make more pots if you want a perfect one]])
