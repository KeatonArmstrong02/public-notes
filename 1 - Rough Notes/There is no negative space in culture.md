# There is no negative space in culture

Culture has no opposite. Because of what we see when [[Looking at the negative space]] we can recognize how anything that we could potentially see as moving away from a specific aspect of culture is in the end, building a new culture to counter the space.

Me and Kendall had conversations about the beauty standards. With the woke agendas I saw, all we had the option of doing is creating a new culture where we celebrate something new instead. Remember that [[Culture compounds]] so it becomes a weird slippery slope of because you "celebrate" one thing, now you are excluding the other.
