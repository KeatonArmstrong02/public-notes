# The Depressed Viewpoint
A lot of the things that happen to us is very difficult to cope with. We have no idea how to even get our head around it. 
When I was depressed for so long as a boy I had no idea why I was so sad. I fought to hard to bury some of the things that happened to me. Scarily enough I succeeded.

I remember wishing for something bad to happen to me. I'd actually felt a tiny bit of relief when Jacob had died. I felt as if my depression wasn't justified. It was like I still needed a *reason* to be so sad not realizing what I'd already had was certainly enough.

------

We wish so much for the terrible thing to have never happened. That this is the only conceivable world in which we could be happy. Yet we know theres nothing to do about it.
We never realized the other option. The more accurate perspective of the world in which we would make peace with what has happened to us.

The depressed person still has an inaccurate view of the world, they still expect much better or much worse (their current place).
