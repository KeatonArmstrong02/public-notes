When we're young, we may focus on "when I grow up" you're looking forward to your young adult years. The shadow you cast is pointing towards that point in your life.

If we get much older, and still focus on this point when we get past it, the shadow we cast is backwards. If you hold on to the focus for a very long time, you will cast a very long, and dark shadow over your timeline.

Focus where you are. Cast the light over yourself
