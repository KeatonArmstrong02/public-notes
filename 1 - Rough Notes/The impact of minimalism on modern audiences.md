[[Modern audiences]] are heavily catered to. In marketing, this becomes [[The arms race of social psychology]]. Combined with minimalism, this changes the landscape of art.

With so much input and volume audiences have infinite choice, we will choose what is entertaining to us, not difficult to understand or boring. Minimalism values digestibility, be bare and simple. Make messages quick, efficient, and clear. However, this creates an economy which bottoms out stupidity. If something is complex, too deep to understand, then audiences consider it bad art. 

"Difficult writing is bad writing, because the author was unclear" I agree in the sense of the trend of minimalism, however I would disagree when it comes to genuine quality. Maybe your reader is stupid, and you can't possibly control that.

In Brave New World, Mustapha Mond talks about this exact thing "Of course (Othello) it's good, but no one would understand it." We sacrificed high art for social stability.
