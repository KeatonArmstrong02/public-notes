# Optimization is spiritually ineffective

This is the obsession with optimization combined with modern narratives and our attempts at escaping them.

This is [[The Existential Frame]] but now it's life-maxxing. (Fit as much as you can into your life.)

I think it's ineffective because by chopping off the strange "In-efficient" bits of our lives to try and make it all work, we forget the human limitations. What we plan for practicality we lose on the slow breath of spirituality. We think we can just coerce our souls into shape as we need them to.

When we over-optimize, we lose [[Serendipital gravity]]

Optimization is a fad of obsessing over the details. It is maximalism towards effectiveness in some domains, but to the detriment of other's. This is the 2-hour rain-dance morning routine to prepare for work when you could just work. This is bio-hacking yourself into looking the same as a hospital patient.
