# The destination of algorithms

Getting exactly what you want.

The problem with social media algorithms is that they are extremely good at feeding us exactly what we want.

This is inherent to humans, but the challenge presented to those of us who wish to be better thinkers, we are always attempting to move beyond bias, or at the least work with them. We cannot thrive in a circumstance where all bias is confirmed. This, is exactly the world presented to us with these algorithms.


In a world of intelligent tools, the naive users become fools for the machine to play. If you don't care to hold your own reigns, the algorithms sure would love to take them for you.
