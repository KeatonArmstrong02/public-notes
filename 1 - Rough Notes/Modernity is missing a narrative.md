# Modernity is missing a narrative

Think of what stories we heard growing up. What ideas we were sold or attempted to be sold. We're merely told what to do, or be, but that we can decide anything. It's not a compelling narrative.

This leads us into what me and Kieran experienced as "Half-assing life until you finally fuck it up enough to justify killing yourself." It's a death of silent sabotage and slow suffocation. Of isolating yourself just enough so the one thing you've had your entire life, "Potential" is snuffed out. Then it's okay. Then you're finally worthless to modern society.
