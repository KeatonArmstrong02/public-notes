**Hypocrisy as a fear must be discarded**

The tendency by others to hold the idea that you are a hypocrite because you said one thing and later contradicted it with action. Are they incapable of change? Of unfolding the previous context?

"Sometime's a hypocrite is just a man in the process of changing"  - Dalinar Kolin

To be sure, it is one thing to break the integrity of your character. To be dishonest. To lie in the way of presenting yourself in light while degrading yourself in the dark.

Unchanging word is a nice fantasy, it would mean we could always trust it. It is transparent and requires nothing else.

but changing is reality. Under change, required cohesion with a past version of yourself is a recipe for stunted growth. It's who you were tyrannizing who you could be in the future.

Humans have states and emotions, we are not machines, we have many layers to call that each thing we say must remain unbroken is a ludicrous idealism which breaks all ability for us to iterate.


For if a man has changed, it is his duty to act out with courage; his honest hypocrisy.


Interested to see this in a semi-historical context. Societies which valued Honor seem most to defame others by claiming them as hypocrites or cowards. We see it of the greeks.

Honor demands you remain true and unchanging. Honesty demands you be you, it is closer to human nature. Though at times I suppose in history it was more divine to seem this way.
