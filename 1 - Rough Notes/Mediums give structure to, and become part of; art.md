# Mediums give structure to art

"Adobe _Illustrator_ is essentially different from any of the individual tools _Illustrator_ contains. Such a medium creates a powerful immersive context, a context in which the user can have new kinds of thought, thoughts that were formerly impossible for them."

*Mediums shape how we engage with reality*, while tools help us act within that framework.

The thing by which, and through which, something is done. [[Mediums are the means of expression]]

Martial arts schools are mediums:
The art is expressing violence through the body
- Jiu Jitsu is trying to suppress the violence others are capable of by smothering their body
- Muy Thai is effective at damaging another body

The art of dance is expressing through movements of the body
Schools of dance will be the mediums
One might try to optimize which schools they follow based on the art that they like, how they'd like to express in that way.

The medium may just as much inspire us as the expression that is behind, and coming through it.

Plays and movies both tell stories but movies have tools to manipulate time and edit scenes.
