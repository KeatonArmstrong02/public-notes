Designer told us we were going to the little blue planet they discovered centuries ago

"Won't the planet be totally different by time we get there?"

The gamelek empire has been in power far longer than any other in the history of life. Ours was the first planet to form within the conditions for life. We were also the first to break free from the shackled existence within those prisons we called planets.

---

Without the resources to spend, designer told us he had another plan. He was going to inseminate a new gene they discovered onto the planet.

Those with the gene would be.. different, they would lack to restrictions on the will to power just as we had broken out of centuries ago. Emotions are a scourge on consciousness really, a flaw in the design.

Psychopathy.
