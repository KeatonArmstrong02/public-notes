On Credibility in writing:
> That’s a journalistic trick which you can also apply to literature. For example, if you say that there are elephants flying in the sky, people are not going to believe you. But if you say that there are four hundred and twenty-five elephants flying in the sky, people will probably believe you. _One Hundred Years of Solitude_ is full of that sort of thing...

> The problem for every writer is credibility. Anybody can write anything so long as it’s believed.
