Welcome to my first website! You'll see there are a few features here that may not be typical of a blog. 

That's because this isn't a blog, this is a digital garden.

I decided upon this design to allow for more exploration within the site rather than top-down structure. I can publish directly from my Obsidian vault, allowing me to continue tending to the garden as I write.

There won't be as much polish, and sometimes my notes won't be super clear, that's because the experience is designed to keep the rough edges and structure of the way I think. Not necessarily being read-able to others.
