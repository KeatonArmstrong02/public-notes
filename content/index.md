👋 Hi, I'm Keaton, welcome to my website! 

This is where I learn in public and post my notes which don't make it onto my [substack](https://keatonarmstrong.substack.com/).

You'll notice that this isn't a typical blog, it's a [[Digital Garden]].

You can navigate into clusters of notes with the graph view, each of the dots represent a note and have a visual for how they are connected to one another. The more lines, the more an idea is connected to others.

I decided upon this design to allow for a more exploratory user experience. Rather than a birds-eye, we're on street-view. This build with Quartz allows me to publish directly from Obsidian, so users get a mirrored experience of how I write and navigate the notes myself.

My notes follow the principles of the [[Zettelkasten]].
Read about my experience adopting the system in [this essay](https://keatonarmstrong.substack.com/p/how-to-waste-everything-you-learn?r=1to3gv)
