👋 Hi, I'm Keaton, welcome to my website! 

This is where I learn in public and post my notes which don't make it onto my [substack](https://keatonarmstrong.substack.com/).

You'll notice that this isn't a typical blog, it's a [[Digital Garden]].

I decided upon this design to allow for a more exploratory user experience. Rather than birds-eye, we're on street-view. This build with Quartz allows me to publish directly from my Obsidian. So users will get a mirrored experience my notes as I write and navigate through them myself.

More on this design decision see here:
[[]]

My notes follow the principles of the [[Zettelkasten]]. This organic way of structuring my notes gives me free access to create first and organize later, rather than be stuck in indexing hell.

You'll can navigate into clusters of notes with the graph view, each of the dots represent a note and have a visual for how they are connected to one another. The more lines, the more an idea is connected to others.
