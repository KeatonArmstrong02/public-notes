# Judging skill with emotions too early

Don't be in a rush to make a judgement whether you like a new skill early on.
Dillon said, I don't decide whether I like something or not until I'm good at it.

When you're at "I don't know what I don't know" or even more unbalanced, "I know what I need to know but don't know it yet" You're going to be overwhelmed.
