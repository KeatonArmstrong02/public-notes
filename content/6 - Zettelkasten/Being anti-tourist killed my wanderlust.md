# Being anti-tourist killed my wanderlust

In Europe I tried to slum so much and not be a "tourist" but it's so build up and trying to be counter to everything was just a bad idea. Allow yourself to do what makes you happy, don't just crowdsource or go counter to crowdsource. The answer will be somewhere in the middle.

Not to mention the amount of time I was spending in a rush didn't allow me to [[Cast the adventure net]].
