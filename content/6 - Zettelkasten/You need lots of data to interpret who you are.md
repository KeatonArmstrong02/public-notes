# You need lots of data to interpret who you are

Trying to know who you are at a young age, particularly right after school is going to be hard, not because you've done anything wrong you just dont have data.

Perhaps you've had the big events which become core pieces but you need more nuanced experiences, find these values. Find what you don't believe to be true.
