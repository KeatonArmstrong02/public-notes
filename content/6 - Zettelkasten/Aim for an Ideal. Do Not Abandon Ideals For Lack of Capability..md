# Aim for an Ideal. Do Not Abandon Ideals For Lack of Capability.

Whatever you see as an ideal outcome allows every decision on the road to it gets to be binary. You’re moving towards or away. It gives you the simplicity to be able to act. Then the faults and chaos of life will still throw you off whenever it whims, but you won’t be the reason, when you’re at your best, for why you aren’t where you want to be.
