# The personal is universal

"One man's journey is every man's journey." - Paulo Coelho

Many artists have discovered a similar concept.

John Mayer mentions, "whenever I try to write something to fill the entire galaxy...I've never gotten a song that way. But if I write a song about something the size of a cup of water, and I do it right--I notice a week later that it's got the universe in it."
