# The zeigarnik effect

The Zeigarnik effect is a [[Psychological tool]] we can use for our [[Productivity]].

"A task is easier to complete, when it is left incomplete."

This takes advantage of the open loop in our brain, the inner completionist that wants to finish a task is stronger than the procrastinator who doesn't want to start the task.

For best use, don't look at a task as something to complete. Rather, see it as something to start so you can have it incomplete.

The opposite of procrastination is *doing something* so let doing something be the real goal.

Completion is just a bonus that we get motivated towards once we start.
