# On other's comments about you

You might want to listen at times, it is info, but understanding credibility is more important than whether you *like* the info or not.

The duty of free thinking, is that you get to decide ultimately if it is accurate or not. If you've decided it isn't, great continue on.
Yet if you recognize accuracy, you'll address it. Not have to change it or apologize for anything, but at least address it.
