'The man who is incapable of being bad, cannot be praised for his goodness"
I'd challenge this, then what about a great child raised into his innocence who would never conceive of doing such things? We say he is capable, would he need to prove it or ever get the power?


Don't get lost in becoming a warrior that you forget you are to protect yourself. Not Kill
