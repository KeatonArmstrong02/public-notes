# Energy without direction

Energy needs to be focussed to a point in order to be effective.

Many of the things we want to do, or move, have a static friction coefficient that is higher than if it were moving.

Unfocused workers with [[General ambition]] are typically working *really* hard, they're just dividing their focus onto too many items so they dont break the static threshold for anything. They don't make progress.
