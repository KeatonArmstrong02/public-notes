# Life is your responsibility

Life is not our decision. We did not choose life. Life is something that has happened to us. It is not our *fault*. [[Fault vs Responsibility]].
We have been blessed with a body, intelligence, and a capability.
My only business is to see that it happens at the highest point. I must reach a peak within me.

Right now the only only only thing you own is life. The rest is all just an assumption.
