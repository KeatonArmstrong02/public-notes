# Don't seek to become more, become less

Instead of trying to become more--through an idealized vision--become less of what you are now.

Shave and rattle the pieces of you that conflict with your core-self. Challenge the assumptions you've lived with your whole life because father said, "you must"

---
ex.
There’s a weird paradoxical feeling with self-actualization. While you may seek change within to move towards an ideal of ourselves, I only feel as if I’m shaving off the invasive parts. The parts of me that challenged my core and left it more obscure.

Instead of becoming more, I’m becoming less.

----

Become the absolute authority within your own life. Play by your rules [[The rules you play by are subject to who you want to become]].
