# If you don’t feel awkward, you’re learning at a snail’s pace

Awkward is a barrier to learning but also essential for accelerated learning.
Because [[Understanding requires effortful engagement]] that engagement will feel hard. You can't expect to learn well while in ease the entire time. This is studied in the [[Misinterpreted-effort hypothesis]]. This is because when our brain is forming new connections it is having to work hard. This is the awkwardness, it is also the forming of new pathways i.e. Learning.
