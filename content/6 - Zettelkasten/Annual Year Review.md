#### Sahil Bloom's 7 Questions

1. **What did I change my mind about this year?**
• What "Software updates" did I have this year?

2. **What Created energy this year?**
• What activities, projects, people *created* energy in my life?
• Did I spend ample time on these *energy creators* in 2024 or did I neglect them
Goal: Spend more time on them

3. **What Drained energy this year?**
• What activities, projects, people *drained* energy in my life?
• Did I allow the energy drainers to persist or did I manage them in real-time?
Goal: Spend less time on them

4. **What were the boat anchors in my life?**
• Self-limiting beliefs and stories
• Self-sabotaging behavior
• Bad habits that cut into growth

5. **What did I not do because of fear?**
• What was the downside if I had taken action?
• What was the upside if I had taken action?

6. **What were my greatest hits and worst misses this year?**
• After list, why the hits hit and the misses missed

7. **What did I learn this year?**
