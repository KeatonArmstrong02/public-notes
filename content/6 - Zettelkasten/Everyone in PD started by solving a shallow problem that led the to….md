My shallow problem was: how can I be happy? What is this life? What am I supposed to do with it?

This included Health, Wealth, Purpose(Most of all), Spiritual investigation, curiosity digging, making friends and building relationships, improving EVERY aspect.

So my purpose?
I want to teach others how to be human.
Because I didn’t always know how. I didn’t always accept it, I didn’t always “Live”
My life is all I’ve ever owned and now I value it incredibly. It wasn’t always like that

The ultimate generalist, I want to explore what it means to be human. What is it that we experience?
