# Conversation as a generative drafting tool.


Notice the difference between typing and writing with a pen.

Different tools give you different speeds, which I’ve come to find has a different result of sophistication or edit-ability.

Conversation can wildly flow from one idea to the next, it is low-energy to produce and doesn't have a requirement aside from engagement.

You get to revisit ideas, quickly try out building different castles and well timed questions can help us find chinks.

It is "Teaching" the idea, seeing if it transfers
