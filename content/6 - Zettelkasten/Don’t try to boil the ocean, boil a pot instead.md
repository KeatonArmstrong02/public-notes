# Don’t try to boil the ocean, boil a pot instead

Heat dissipates.

The ocean carries a lot of molecules which heat can dissipate into, energy is absorbed by all around it.

So if you want to heat water up as much as possible, boil a small pot. You won't get anywhere with the ocean.
