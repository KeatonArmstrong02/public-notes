# Get the most bucks

You can't always optimize to get the most bang for your buck. When the goal of the game is to get the most bucks, sometimes you just gotta get the most bucks. Screw your efficiency and optimization. 80/20 only gets you to 80, if you want 100 or 120 you need to do the 80% more to squeeze out another 20.

If I want to save up as much money as possible quickly, I have to drop pretenses of Efficiency and $ per hour and just work as many hours as possible.

In contrast, this idea can lead to quick burnout. I mainly have it because I think it's very different than my default. Sometimes I need a reminder.
#### Reference
This makes me think of all the times I've tried to plan my life ahead, to try to "efficiently" catch as much as I can with life. To load the senses up. The contrast to solve the problems it comes with is to [[Reverse engineer your regrets]]
