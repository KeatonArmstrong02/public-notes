# The psychological load of philosophy
There seems to be a kind of weight associated with different truths in the world, at times you can't lift it because it isn't tethered down enough with experience?

At the end of the day you must be able to handle the psychological load of whatever you believe in. To have the capacity to accept all of it or you’ll crumble.
