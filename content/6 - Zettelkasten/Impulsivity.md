# Impulsivity
An aspect of [[Extraversion]]
Impulsivity is the opposite of overthinking, it is not putting any brakes on your actions. Thoughts are impulsive, if we always follow them that is impulsivity.

Individuals with higher impulsivity are much more likely to be addicted to gambling.

I've seen its benefit within extraversion for making friends. See [[On Playfulness]]
