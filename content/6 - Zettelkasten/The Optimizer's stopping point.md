# The Optimizer's stopping point


#### Reference


I often find myself in this loop
- Become Interested in something
- Research
- Watch experts
- (Turns into entertainment)
- “Learning” phase taking notes on trying to create a perfect system
- Excited at the idea but generally overwhelmed by the end goal that I’ve seen
- Doesn’t take action and moves on or takes a little bit of action, it gets hard, then I stop
