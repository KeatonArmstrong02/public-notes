Simple answers are lids to complex jars

#thinking 

The depth of simplicity is like the Tardis

It’s infinitely large on the inside, but from the outside it’s just a simple blue box

What seems so small, actually holds the universe inside. Keep chasing that rabbit hole and there’s infinite depth. The only way out is the simple lid to keep us from wandering.

It’s a balancing act between the two, complexity and simplicity

Artists will use this concept to speak deeply while on the surface, presenting a small-contained story [[The personal is universal]].
