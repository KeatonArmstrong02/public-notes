# 2 Games of Tennis

Ramo, a prominent physicist who took to improving his tennis late in life, observed that there are two versions of tennis.

In amateur tennis, roughly 80% of points are lost. These come from unforced errors.

In professional tennis, roughly 80% of points are won. A great shot; which the other player cannot return.

With these stats in mind, the best way to win in most levels of tennis is simply by *keeping the ball in play*. Your opponent will occasionally hit a good shot but will mostly lose points by failing to return a hit.

**Most games in life are competing at amateur level. All you need to do is keep the ball in play.**

#### Reference
*Extraordinary Tennis for the Ordinary Player* - Simon Ramo
