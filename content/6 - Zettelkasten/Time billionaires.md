# Time billionaires
Our most valuable resource,*Time*. It is perfectly finite, the time we spend we cannot get back. It is not reproduced or earned.

because of the ticking clock, we are most wealthy at birth and our fortune begins to decay.

1 Billion seconds is ~32 years. We are all Time Billionaires. 

The only thing we can do is invest it and hope to gain other worthwhile rewards.

Understand just how fortunate we are, and spend it wisely.
