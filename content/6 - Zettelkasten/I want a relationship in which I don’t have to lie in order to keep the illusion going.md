Not lying involves she knowing all of who you are. All of what you’ve done, all of what you plan to do, all of what you think.

If it’s all on the table, then you only need act in accordance with this version of you. Tell her when things change, when you do different things and it won’t contradict. But as soon as you want her to believe something that isn’t the truth you would begin to lie
