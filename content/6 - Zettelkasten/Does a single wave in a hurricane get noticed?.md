#modernity 

Accomplishments are celebrated in noise
But character is shown during those quiet moments.

It’s not, who are the “thousands” that read my death on a headline.

But the 3 friends who get together to pour whiskey over my grave. 
Who share stories about me ‘till their faces hurt with laughter.

It’s about their grandkids who see a picture of us on the shelf and ask, “Who is that man?”
