# Fundamental attribution error fallacy

This is the tendency to overemphasize actions others take as attributed to their personality or "how they are" while ignoring or not giving enough weight to the context of a situation.

We see behaviors from others as their character, whereas if we commit the same behavior we know that it’s not all there is to us.

Give others that same leeway you give yourself.

I also feel a bit of comfort in knowing this, as it helps to not throw everyone into a bucket. If someone is rude you don't have to be upset because you can [[lean into our ignorance]] and assume better.

A lot of defensiveness could come from ourselves potentially if worried about how others perceive us.
