# Plowing Traction

First activity to do in the morning. If you can't make time now, you won't make time later.
This first few hours is for your most important task. Your highest priority. The thing you always "wish" you had time for but can't seem to make it. You force it in early.

It will require the sacrifice of other activities. That's okay. Add more to your life by only doing the high quality activities.
