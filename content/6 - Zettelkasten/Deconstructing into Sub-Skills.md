# Deconstructing into Sub-Skills
The mountain is too big to digest, and there is no such skill as "Building a House" or "Judgement" you have to break them down into their components.

We see learning a language as this nebulous mountain of a task, but really it's just Vocabulary and Grammar as the main blocks, a little bit of phonetics, and whichever other features will be in there.
