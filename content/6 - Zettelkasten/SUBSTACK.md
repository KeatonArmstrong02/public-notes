# SUBSTACK

As long as your biases aren’t fallacy, **own it.**

We know open mindedness is paramount to becoming a better thinker, yet it’s sooooo much harder in practice than we think.

When we make a judgement who’s to say you’ll have the motivation to come to a different conclusion or ever recognize it as questionable in the first place?

So instead of the pipe dream of becoming a “pure unbiased thinker”

Get friends in your corner who aren’t afraid to disagree with you.

This way, instead of fighting with your own logic you can just fight them.
