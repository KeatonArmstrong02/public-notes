# Atomic notes

Atomic notes are notes that have consolidated into a fundamental unit of knowledge. They are the building block for this [[Zettelkasten]].

If we stick to the idea that [[Atomic Notes should be concept-based]], we have clear and concise access points.

It has clear boundaries and does not spread or mix too much with other ideas. This lets us [[Prefer associative links > indexed files]] and keep [[High fidelity association]] because the notes are free to mix with each other without fluff or non-applicable association.

We can freely build from the ground up and allow our knowledge to accumulate ([[Knowledge systems should accrete]]).
