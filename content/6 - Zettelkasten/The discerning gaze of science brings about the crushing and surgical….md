# Our only salvation is admittance to ignorance.


Scientific thought, in its true and unabridged form.
Capitalism as well. 
These empires of progress demand of us alignment
Or at the very least to not fall prey to the ugly versions of them.
Do not imagine human nature can be distilled into all good even among seemingly crystalline theories we find ways to corrupt.

The problem is not our weakness perhaps but in the fact that we are becoming the gods of the past.

[[Sapiens by Noah Yuval Harari]]
