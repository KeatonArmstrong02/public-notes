# Survival in the modern world


And it’s not physical anymore in the first world

Then you need a strong sense of self. A strong story to tie you to your own existence

Or else we’ll get captured by the grand fictions that surround us today, and be swallowed up in service to all of these things “bigger” than our own small selves


With infinite information, you need to learn to sort it. To tune most of it out and find the signal within the noise.
