# Beer friends, Wine friends, Whiskey friends

**Beer Friends:** These are the casual, fun-loving friends. You call them up to make an activity better. Perhaps you share a hobby or interest and you enjoy their company. Being with them is always lighthearted.


**Wine Friends:** These friends are a bit deeper and more intimate. You have many stimulating conversations. You share some philosophy, growth, wisdom, or other abstract interests. Perhaps you call on each other for advice from time to time on a particular subject. You don't do many activities together, you probably don't share hobbies or find it hard to want to do much else besides talk with them.


**Whiskey friends:** These are your most profound and enduring connections. These are the friends who have stood beside you during life events and you have a deep understanding of one another. They challenge you, provide wisdom, and emotional security.
