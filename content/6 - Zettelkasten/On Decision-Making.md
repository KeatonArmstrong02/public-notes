# On Decision-Making

Like the greatest **Decision** any of us will make is whether the universe is friendly or foe. Choices require the right headspace to make the right choice.

Sometimes we require strength, momentum, lack of fear, or sobriety to make the right choices.

The more empowered and sober you can be the greater chance of always being in the right space to make those correct choices.

Correct choices lead to a better life lived. Decision-making is everything


How do we decide what future we will follow?

At the beginning all we can do is follow the suggestions of other people. It is only after following what you thought was right do you eventually stumble on something that *feels* right. You could be mistaken, or believe you were mistaken afterwards because this can, and will change. However only by pursuing this intuition of what feels right will we be able to gain new perspective and take a further step into clarity.

Almost as if we’re being led my the head through a maze. When we follow our intuition we can eventually make it out of the maze, and only then can we actually decide and think for ourselves.
