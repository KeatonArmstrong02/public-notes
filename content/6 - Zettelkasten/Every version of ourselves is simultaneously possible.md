# Every version of ourselves is simultaneously possible

There’s nothing worth exploring in partying if you seek to be in a position where you don’t need to.

There’s nothing worth exploring in doubts that pop in your head, because you already know exactly what you want.

The moment these detracting votes become louder than our voice FOR something we slide away. 

Empower the votes you take for the one you wish to elect with [[Identity elections]].

If we vote among too many identities, no one version of us will have solidarity in victory.
#### Reference

Inspired by [[The Midnight Library by Matt Haig]] I've always struggled with the whole concept of self-actualization and how much it means to be us.
