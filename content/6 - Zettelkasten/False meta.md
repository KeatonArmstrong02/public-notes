# False meta

A false meta is when players engage in a game where the "winning strategy" is based on short term one upping and games.

This is the feeling of "playing games" people will talk about within relationships.

Whereas the true game of a relationship is to keep playing, the false meta is adapted by those who do not wish to play infinitely, they are breaking the rule of the infinite game by keeping score.
