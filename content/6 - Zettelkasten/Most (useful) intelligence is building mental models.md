# Most (useful) intelligence is building mental models

These are the visualizations, analogies, diagrams, rules, and metaphors we use to distill complex and abstract ideas into more digestible, and actionable information

Mastery of information is the ability to evaluate. To judge the information because you understand how things interplay together.

Asking "What is the best way to learn" is like asking what is the best move in chess, or the best tool to build a house. It's much more complex of a topic than can be answered with such a question.
