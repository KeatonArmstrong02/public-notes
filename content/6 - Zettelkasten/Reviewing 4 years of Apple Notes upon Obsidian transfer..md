# Reviewing 4 years of Apple Notes upon Obsidian transfer.

I've found multiple multiple sets of duplicate notes. Notes which are the same topic or mixed set of ideas. Most the time the format was horrible and no one else (hardly even me) could understand it on its own.

Metaphors I adapted for use in my life such as; ambition/responsibility, What is a man; bonfire dreams; scripts and questions I liked; I need to do more x; perfectionism; imposter syndrome; Overcompensating weaknesses into strengths; Experience vs Theory; Drafts of things I *could* do in my life; Greatness vs goodness; The "most" important thing;

Why did I have so many copies?

I never reviewed my notes or had been able to refine them or add tot he same one. All I did was capture so my only thinking was in my head, not on a page.

This was a huge inbox of ideas with *zero* editing.

I also evolved over time, note themes which i frequented when I was 19-20 are totally different than what I thought about in the last year.
