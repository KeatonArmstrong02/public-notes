# High fidelity association

Because we [[Prefer associative links > indexed files]] we need to strengthen the bond between the links.

Higher fidelity association is answering the question: "Why is this information analogous to this other link?"

Ask questions like: 
• How does this relate to the other idea?
• Why did I think of this?
• Is there a better analogy I could make?

We immediately think of what relates in our mind, but this practice makes our links much stronger.
