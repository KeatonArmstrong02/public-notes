# General ambition
Ambition is a fire on your ass telling you to go, it's a fire telling you to consume, to acquire. To get shit done.

Without direction, it is a wildfire that consumes you. Exhausting your avenues before they have time to be nurtured into proper pathways.

Acting on this fire alone is burnout, it is kinetic death.

Before you stoke this fire, you must have it controlled. You do this by removing pathways to control the burn. See [[Focus needs more elimination than addition]]

General ambition is a strong form of [[Energy without direction]].
