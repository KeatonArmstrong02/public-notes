# Looking at the negative space
For most things, there isn't ever a state of non-existence. The opposite of talking is silence, which doesn't make the sending of any message negative space.

There is no non-existence of culture, if you are fighting it, you are replacing it. There's no human cloud with which it has dead-space, only the negative space of something else.


Insecurity and security. Psychologists remark, it's not what an athlete has that makes them more obsessed than others, it is typically that they *Lack* something that most other people have.

It is common metaphor within humans to say we have a 'hole' inside us which cannot be filled in the soul. Yet this is just negative space, not non-existence I would argue. The insecure person is trying so obsessively hard to "fill the hole" that sometimes they will build a skill or do something incredibly grand in trying to do so.
