# Boundaries

A boundary is a *statement of a limit you are setting.* 
You communicate what you are going to do and it requires NO action from the other party.


“If you continue to jump on the couch I’m going to come over there and take you off of it”


Commonly conflated with Requests and plain out [Saying No]

Dr. Becky Kennedy Tim Ferriss Podcast
