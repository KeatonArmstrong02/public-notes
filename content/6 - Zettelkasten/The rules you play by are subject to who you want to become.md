# The rules you play by are subject to who you want to become

Something to keep in mind if you're floating around the internet taking in advice, is where it is coming from and what direction it points you in.

If the advice is the content, what you want as a result becomes the context.
When anyone can say anything, we need the context to recognize the message. 

What seems like a good message, applied into a different context could be disastrous. I think humans have a tendency to thing messages must be invulnerable, we can barely see context in high definition. I also thing its an easy way to criticize any message, is find the context in which the message in now something perceived as awful. This is why we see so much political idealism and "Pristine-ism"
