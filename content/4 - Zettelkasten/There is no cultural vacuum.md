Culture has no opposite which could imply non-existence. It has [[Negative space]] we can recognize how anything that we could potentially see as moving away from a specific aspect of culture is in the end, building a new culture to counter the space.

In conversations about beauty standards, I see that there is no absence of, or true "removing" of a standard, there is only replacement. In moving away from some things we will inevitably move towards others. I also see that [[Culture compounds]] so it becomes a weird slippery slope of because you "celebrate" one thing, now you are excluding the other.
