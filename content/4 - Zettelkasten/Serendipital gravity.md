# Serendipital gravity

Quite similar to "The surface area of luck" and [[Cast the adventure net]], this is the idea that in order to encounter serendipity, we rather need to make ourselves easy targets for an encounter.

You don't go looking for rare mobs just anywhere, you go the where they're most likely found.
