# The art of synthesizing ideas

Be mindful not to overly steal, this is how you become an artist about it.

With so many synthesizers, the whole space becomes synthetic; unnatural.

Not that I blame it, there are so many great ideas already out there that to discard them would be a waste.


Synthesis is a metamorphic process of Ideas
2 Ideas + Pressure, Heat, and Time = New idea

Trying to make an original idea from one source alone is too obvious.
