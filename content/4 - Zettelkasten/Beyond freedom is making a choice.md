# Beyond freedom is making a choice

What happens once we're free?

In career, we choose an option that will get us the most access.
In relationships we seek to be single to "keep options open"

but eventually we find something that we deem worth caring about. Worth investing a significant portion of our lives to. The best investors know that hoarding resources is the worst thing to do with them. There is no hoarding time or freedom, you must spend it on the things that are worth doing.
