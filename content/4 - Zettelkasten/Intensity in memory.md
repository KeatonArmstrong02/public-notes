Our brain is better at remembering the intense. Strong emotion, imagery, sound, or tactile sensations are *stickier* in our minds. This is particularly useful when constructing mneumonics as seemingly wackier, or more ultra-precise "special effects" (as called in MB) have more staying power as artificial memories.
