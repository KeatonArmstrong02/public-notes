# Tone in Storytelling & Writing

Those out of disdain or from any perspective outside of love may not mull over well with others. It’s okay, just recognize it and don’t be surprised if you have an unexpected reaction

Tone will change the connotation of a message.

In writing it is the words you use towards the reader.

Are you demanding, friendly, inquisitive, a friend telling a good story?

Know your reader can tell if you don't like people. No one who hates people writes anything good.

Tone is something I want to explore more, perhaps with writing about the same topic and different tones, as a way of deliberating out the voice. Ben Franklin supposedly did the same with "impersonation letters" written from another's point of view.
This would be particularly good for fiction writing and short story in someone else's head.
