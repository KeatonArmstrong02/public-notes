# Lagging measures

Pay attention to many of the things we place value on, they have lagging measures. Particularly with what we can see on the outside, the *Results* of work show no inkling towards the skill, time, or pain that had gone through to produce it.

Competence is a lagging measure of learning.
Physique is a lagging measure of our habits around health.

This is an important concept to think about when we consider [[quartz/content/4 - Zettelkasten/The nature of compounding]]. As it now makes the measure not just lagging, but exponentially lagging.
