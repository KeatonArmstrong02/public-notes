#existential #philosophy #modernity 
It’s larger than your mind.
It pulls you away from everything in your life to prop up this idea, all the while it drains any of your meaning out of you.
You stop caring about your loved ones, your own dreams and desires. 
You give up on outcome.
You give up hope.


Existentialism is wrong.
Not because we KNOW yet
but because I have decided.
