“It's worse to take an interest in irrelevant things than to do nothing"
- Baltasar Gracian

Prioritization is the grand key when it comes to productivity. It's asking what do I spend my time on?

The [[Pareto's Principle]] is a good method to account what tasks you ought to do.
You can also ^2 the Pareto principle until you reach a single specific task.
