A conversation me and Kendall just had.

She values herself on being someone who is forgiving and kind. I saw it as her being a doormat, we both saw the opposite of "Being bullied" as being the bully. But that isn't the case. You can be strong and kind and fair all at the same time, that intersection is boundaries.

I told her about my experiences with Richard(DAVID) and how I noticed only after the fact that boundaries I had were being crossed, regardless of me ever having set them explicitly. This made me realize I either default to "You can treat me this way" or in Kendall's case "They will be mad at me" but the way out was in the middle, a calm assertion that the other person can't do this thing to you, and if they do, you will restrict and change something that you yourself do. It isn't consequences or setting some kind of rule, it's stating an intention for fair play.
