# Learning the wrong lessons

You know what I saw in school? Kids learning really well how to formulate excuses for low effort and weaseling our way out of things. And getting MAD if the teacher didn’t play along and let us off the hook.

The setup of school doesn't reflect reality well.. the lessons we learn in this synthetic environment won't set us up in reality.
