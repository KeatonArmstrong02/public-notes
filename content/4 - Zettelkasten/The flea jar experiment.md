# The flea jar experiment

It's all over the place now but this is my favorite practical example showing the power of [[Limiting beliefs]]. 

Fleas can jump remarkably high, it's part of why they are so damn hard to catch, they basically teleport.

Researchers put a group of fleas into a jar with a closed lid, and after banging their heads too much they realized they couldn't jump as high as they wanted to, there was a physical barrier. They adjusted their jumping to be just below the lid to avoid more brain damage.

Once the fleas were removed, they continued to jump at a maximum height of how high they jumped within the jar. *For the rest of their lives.*

Even though they were out of the jar, they still believed they could only jump so high.

What jars are we holding ourselves in?
