A good cook can make anything as long as he has the right recipe.
But only a master chef understands the art so well that he can break from recipes. He has creative distinction and proven "taste" within his craft. He understands certain rules so well, he knows how to use them to his favor and edit things around.

An amateur lifter may be great at following his routine, or even recognize what he lacks, but the master lifter knows which workouts to fill in for those weaknesses, like a coach he can create the roadmap to getting to where he needs to be.
