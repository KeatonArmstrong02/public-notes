If I had all the knowledge in the world, how would it change what I’m doing?

Besides academic papers or a novel business, I think that very little things would change.

The act of building your life, and having the information you need to do it isn't such a big gap. The gap is in what you have to do to get there.

You could probably learn skills a little bit quicker with your better strategy, but consider how much knowledge contradicts. You'd have to strap in and start doing the thing to learn skills anyways.

Knowledge of yourself beats out any other type here. If you have all the information in the world to pursue, what do you choose? You still have limited time and limited power. Deciding what to throw out when you have so much opportunity requires deep self-knowledge.

I'd still have to write, I'd still have to climb, I'd still have to engage in my relationships and entertain myself.
