# Isolated information is not understood, thereby useless

Because we know [[Insight requires context]], it is up to us to provide more context to ideas we are presented with. [[Knowledge systems should be densely-linked]] so we must try forming [[High-fidelity association]] with new ideas to see how they relate with one another.
