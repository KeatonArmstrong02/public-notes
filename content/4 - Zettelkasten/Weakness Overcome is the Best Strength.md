# Weakness overcome is the best strengths

I sucked at emotion, sucked at explaining because I rambled, and hated how weak I was.
Now they are the things I'm most proud of, emotional understanding, improving my thinking, improving my strength and athleticism.

Tony hated math, he felt stupid for a lot of his life.

You see, despite the results of cards we were dealt, we *cared* that we weren't as natural at something as others seemed to be, lets call this below the average.

If you're average, if you've reached "Good enough" then you'll offload to System 1 and stop paying attention
