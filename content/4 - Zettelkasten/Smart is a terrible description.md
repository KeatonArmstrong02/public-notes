# Smart is a terrible description

Knowing [[quartz/content/4 - Zettelkasten/The foundation of self-esteem]] it is important to understand what labels we build our identity around.

It's more of a comment on [[Circle of Competence]]. Smart isn't an all-knowing description and it's quite dangerous and burdensome what assumptions come with it.


Smart is a [[Lagging measures]] and as the result of work someone has already put in, it's not a highly accurate representation of their current self, it could be work that was put in years ago.
