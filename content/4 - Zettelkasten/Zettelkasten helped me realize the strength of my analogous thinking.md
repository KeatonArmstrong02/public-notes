I used to break into seemingly infinite tangents when I would talk about Ideas because I would dive into their analogous associations within my mind as one train of thought.

Zettelkasten's [[Atomic notes]] solved this problem for me because I was able to whittle away at my tangents until I saw clear stopping points for each idea.

The tangents were like clicking wildly into different links as soon as I made the connection in my head. I tried to cast a bigger and bigger net in my attempts to capture a few ideas and bundle them together. Working on the ideas, making them simpler and smaller, that is what now allows me to bundle them together more nicely.

I still have a wildly associative brain, but my practice within the zettelkasten helps me to articulate my associations and save other people from suffering through my own [[Rough drafters]] speak

It makes so much sense to me now why I was drawn to obsidian and never made good use of other organization structures, sure, I could have taken the time to learn them, but this seems so natural for me. It's closer to the way I think in my head. (Justin Sung's Schema) and Bush Vannevar's associative links
