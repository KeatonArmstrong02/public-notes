I don't think agency is general. Meaning, there are some people who show extraordinary agency in some areas of their lives, then have a devastating lack of it in others.

Entrepreneurs who will break past any barrier to success, yet can't do the same for their family life.

The philosopher who strives to be a free thinker, yet has no bias to action outside of his mental landscape.

I think this is fine. It's something rather obvious when you think about it, perhaps it's that there is only so much time, or individuals won't be as well-rounded as we sometimes wish, whatever it is, even if "*how you do anything is how you do everything*" not everything can be approached the same way. The competitive businessman will only bring this competitive nature into his relationships.
