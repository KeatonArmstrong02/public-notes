# On Journaling

"It will help you remember how you became the person you are today."
- Lavinia Spalding

A journal's best place in the hands of someone who has the habit of writing elsewhere is this:
The spiritual windshield wiper. The reference material for your life and documentation of your mind and who you are becoming over time.

Your life is the most important subject you will ever encounter. TAKE NOTES ON IT.

Journaling will help you [[You Must Find the Truth for Yourself]] and break into [[Freethinking]] by developing a deeper understanding of yourself.

#### Reference
Lavinia Spalding
