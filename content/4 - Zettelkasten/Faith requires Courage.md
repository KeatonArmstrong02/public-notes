## Leaps of Faith

Sometimes we need to act without full knowledge of the outcome. This is a test of courage and with the “blindness” we consider it faith. Because not everyone will believe in you. Some will. 
Some take this leap of faith completely on their own when their spirit is screaming at them.
Others need the encouragement of others, toil and fight with themselves to muster up the courage.
Do not detract from the value displayed here when it is the latter case.
Only 1 man makes the leap. 
You cannot strip the credit of having done the act. Regardless of any outside forces
