I find the true goal to be a mix of immersion and making contact with grammar and words you don’t yet know. 
The “sweet spot” where understanding most of the words, you can learn new ones through context.

It’s a way of building your wins and feeling good about understanding your target language, while also strengthening that mortar that holds it all together. It increases your contact with the language and can greatly improve your thinking speed from reading, and listening.


But remember when you run into the things you don’t yet know, now you must study it to understand better.
