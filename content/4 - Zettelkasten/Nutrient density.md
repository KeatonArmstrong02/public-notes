# Nutrient density


If there's any diet advice that works across the board, it's to prefer nutrient-dense foods over high calorie or ingredient food with low nutritional value.

When we're hungry it's because our body wants nutrients for fuel, but the bad strategy of overeating comes from gorging on low density snack food.
