# On Solo Travel

This is a true experience of adventure, to go off in solitude, forced out of your comfort zone with no familiarity.
Though what you realize isn’t the differences, its actually the commonalities of humans across any culture
We value good people, good times, fun, adventure, we all have our misconceptions and things we don’t understand about each other, but through communication we are more similar than we think.
