I won't say outright that relationship advice is useless. I don't claim that it won't solve things for you either. This is a useful tool to remember when you're "solving" together in your relationship.

Relationship advice might tell you that you should do X, but you and your partner don't really care for it, so why would you listen?

There are so many assumptions that come packaged with society's view of relationships, and there is some undeniable wisdom in there. But my society doesn't know shit about how to be happy. [[America leads the world by over 3x in single-parent households.]] Divorce rates, opposing ideological pressures, depression, anxiety, economic factors, these are just a few to take into consideration the next time some old head tells you "your generation doesn't get it" times have changed. We are struggling.

[[Relationships require creative problem solving]]. There are different types of relationships for different people. The key to making yours work is figuring out who exactly you and your partner are. Then how you function together.

Many social rules are dependent on other factors. Understand what "rules" are compromised because they're just not true anymore or weren't true in the firstplace, or were only true for a certain type of couple.

All of this to say. Screw everyone else's opinion of how you're supposed to do things. Love your partner, talk to them, negotiate, discover, creatively invent, and practice ways to move forward together. Towards happiness, life partnership, excitement, stability, whatever it is you as a couple have decided to value together. 

Your ship is the one you steer, account for the winds, account for the currents, and steer it well.
