#thinking 

Human problem

As generations pass
As times change

The ideal that we follow becomes muddled as new teachers, new interpretations are spread along. 

This isn’t to say more teachers are bad, it’s to only way information is passed along. 

We have no control that the context will change. 

And it is for that reason, interpretations change, definition and meanings muddle up

Until eventually “God” becomes everything
Rather than the the universe’s representation of qualities that man is to follow
