In an existence where opportunity cost is baked in, we don’t get the chance to split test things. By doing one thing you can’t do a different thing.

"If I am exceeding in one aspect of my life, I am most certainly failing in another" - Tiny Experiments

The frame: What regrets can I live with? What is the one regret I can NOT live with? 
“What do I want” can be inaccurate or pull us in too many directions sometimes, so use your “running away from” engine for a bit to just go in the opposite direction. See [[Directionally correct]]


3x more loss aversion than desire

If you can’t get yourself to do something, then think about it from what you have to lose rather than what you have to gain.
