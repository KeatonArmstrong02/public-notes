On meaning
> “The meaning of my existence is that life has addressed a question to me. Or, conversely, I myself am a question which is addressed to the world, and I must communicate my answer, for otherwise I am dependent upon the world’s answer. ” -Carl Jung (Memories, Dreams, Reflections)

> "I am not altogether pessimistic about neurosis. In many cases, we have to say: 'Thank heaven he could make up his mind to be neurotic.' Neurosis is really an attempt at self-cure… It is an attempt of the self-regulating psychic system to restore the balance." -Carl Jung
