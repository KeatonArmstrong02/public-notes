#### Introduction

Self-Esteem has two interrelated aspects:
1. Confidence in our ability to think, *trust our own minds, and cope with the basic requirements of life*. *Self-Efficacy*
2. Confidence in our right to be successful and happy, that we are *worthy and appropriate to existence*. *Self-Respect*

##### Self-Respect
If I respect myself and require that others deal with me respectfully as well, I signal and behave with high self-respect. Others will respond to this.

🚩If I lack self-respect and allow discourtesy, abuse, and negligence I signal low self-respect and permit for others to walk over me and respond to the same regard as myself.


#### 🚨 The Illusion of Esteem:
Low-esteem means low defense against the manipulation of fear in our actions.
"Fear of reality, to which we feel inadequate"

Low esteem is living more to avoid pain than to experience joy. Not good or bad, understand this and how to act accordingly, as it's not the best operating system.

==Self-esteem doesn't *determine* our thinking. It affects our ***emotional incentives*.**==
